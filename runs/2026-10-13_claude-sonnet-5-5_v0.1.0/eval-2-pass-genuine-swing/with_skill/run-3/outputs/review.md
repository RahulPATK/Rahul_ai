## Focus areas
- **APAC is the main mover.** Orders rose from 2,140 to 2,890 (+35.0%), revenue from 88,300 to 121,900 (+38.1%) and active customers from 7,910 to 9,120 (+15.3%). The APAC team has confirmed these numbers are real.
- **APAC drives most of the company growth.** Of the +37,100 GBP revenue increase (462,300 to 499,400), APAC contributes +33,600. It also contributes +750 of the +865 additional orders.
- **Other regions are steady.** North America, EMEA and LATAM each moved by about 1–2% on orders and under 1.5% on revenue. North America revenue is +0.9%, EMEA +0.9% and LATAM +1.4%.
