## Focus areas
- **APAC is the main mover.** Orders rose from 2,140 to 2,890 (+35.0%), revenue from 88,300 to 121,900 (+38.1%) and active customers from 7,910 to 9,120 (+15.3%). The APAC team has confirmed these figures are real.
- **APAC drives most of the company-level growth.** Of the +865 increase in total orders, +750 came from APAC. Of the +37,100 increase in revenue, +33,600 came from APAC. Of the +1,380 increase in active customers, +1,210 came from APAC.
- **Other regions were broadly flat.** North America, EMEA and LATAM each moved by less than 2% on orders and revenue (orders +0.8% to +1.7%, revenue +0.9% to +1.4%).

## Data checks
- All four regions (North America, EMEA, APAC, LATAM) appear in both this week's and last week's data.
- Regional sums match reported_totals.csv for orders, revenue and active customers, for both weeks.
- The APAC swing was confirmed as real with the APAC team before this review was written.
