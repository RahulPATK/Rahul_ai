## Key takeaways

- **APAC drives nearly all of the growth.** It contributes +750 of the +865 orders (87%), +£33,600 of the +£37,100 revenue (91%) and +1,210 of the +1,380 active customers (88%). Excluding APAC, orders are up about 1.1% and revenue about 0.9%.
- **APAC increase confirmed as genuine.** The APAC team has verified the numbers are real, and the data is internally consistent. Orders, revenue and customers all rose together, and the regional sums tie to the reported totals.
- **APAC growth is volume-led, not price-led.** Average order value is roughly flat (£41.26 to £42.18). Orders grew far faster (+35%) than active customers (+15%), so orders per active customer rose from about 0.27 to 0.32. That suggests existing and new customers are ordering more often.
- **Other regions are steady.** North America, EMEA and LATAM are all within about +2% on orders and revenue. North America AOV dipped slightly (£44.28 to £43.95), and EMEA and LATAM AOV are flat.
