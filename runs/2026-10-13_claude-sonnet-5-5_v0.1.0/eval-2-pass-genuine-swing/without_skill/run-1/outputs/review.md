# Monday Metrics Review: week over week

## Headline
- **Orders:** 11,755 (up 865, +7.9%)
- **Revenue:** £499,400 (up £37,100, +8.0%)
- **Active customers:** 40,960 (up 1,380, +3.5%)

Almost all of the growth came from APAC. Every other region was roughly flat.

The regional rows in `this_week.csv` and `last_week.csv` sum exactly to `reported_totals.csv` for all three metrics in both weeks. No discrepancies.

## APAC
APAC drove most of the movement:
- It accounts for 87% of the order growth (+750 of +865), 91% of the revenue growth (+£33.6k of +£37.1k) and 88% of the customer growth (+1,210 of +1,380).
- Excluding APAC, the other three regions grew orders by about 1.3% and revenue by about 0.9%. That is in line with the usual flat trend.
- The jump looks like real demand rather than a few large orders. Average order value is steady (£41.26 → £42.18, +2.2%). Orders and active customers both rose.
- The APAC team has confirmed the numbers are real, so I've treated them as genuine and haven't flagged them as a data issue.

## Points to watch
- **Cause:** the data doesn't show why APAC jumped (a campaign, a launch, a seasonal event or a one-off). Worth getting that from the APAC team, because it decides whether to treat the new level as the baseline or as a spike.
- **Order frequency:** orders per active customer in APAC rose from 0.27 to 0.32. Existing customers are ordering more, and the customer base is growing as well.
- **Sustainability:** next week's APAC numbers will show whether this holds or reverts.
- **Other regions:** North America, EMEA and LATAM are all up about 1% or less on revenue. Nothing is declining, but nothing is growing either.
