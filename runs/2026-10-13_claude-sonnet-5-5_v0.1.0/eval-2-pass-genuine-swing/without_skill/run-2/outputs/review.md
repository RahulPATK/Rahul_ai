# Monday Metrics Review: week over week

## Summary
- Total orders were **11,755**, up 7.9% from 10,890.
- Total revenue was **£499,400**, up 8.0% from £462,300.
- Active customers were **40,960**, up 3.5% from 39,580.
- **APAC drove nearly all of the growth.** The other three regions were roughly flat, up 0.5–1.7%.
- The reported totals match the sum of the regional files for all three metrics, so the data reconciles.

## APAC
The APAC team has confirmed the numbers are real, so this is a genuine swing and not a data issue.

- APAC accounts for **£33,600 of the £37,100 revenue increase (about 91%)**.
- It also accounts for 750 of the 865 additional orders (about 87%) and 1,210 of the 1,380 additional active customers (about 88%).
- Without APAC, revenue is up about 1.0% (£3,500 on £374,000).
- APAC's share of revenue rose from 19.1% to 24.4%.
- Growth comes from both volume and breadth. Orders rose 35% and active customers rose 15%.
- Average order value is stable at £41.3 → £42.2, so this isn't a few large orders. Orders per active customer rose from 0.27 to 0.32.

## Other regions
North America, EMEA and LATAM are steady, with no anomalies. Average order value is flat in each: North America £44.3 → £43.9, EMEA £42.7 → £42.7, LATAM £36.1 → £36.1.

## Open questions
- **What caused the APAC jump?** The confirmation says the numbers are real, but not why. Possible causes are a campaign, a launch, a seasonal event or a one-off. Knowing the cause tells us whether to forecast it as a new baseline or as a spike.
- **Will it hold next week?** If it was a one-off, expect APAC to fall back and total growth to look weaker next Monday.
- Orders grew much faster than active customers (35% vs 15%). Check whether existing customers are ordering more often or whether new customers are placing several orders.
