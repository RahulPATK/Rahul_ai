## Key points

- **North America** contributed the most in absolute terms (+£4.8k revenue, +145 orders) and is the largest region at about 41% of revenue.
- **APAC and LATAM** posted the fastest growth, at +3.7% and +4.0% on revenue and +4.2% and +5.2% on orders. LATAM is the smallest region, so its absolute contribution is small (+£1.4k).
- **EMEA** fell on every metric: revenue -£3.1k (-2.0%), orders -90 (-2.5%) and customers -80 (-0.6%). Its revenue decline is smaller than its order decline, so AOV edged up slightly (£42.71 to £42.92). The drop looks like lower volume and fewer customers, not lower basket size. The data doesn't show the cause.
- Without EMEA's decline, total revenue would have grown by about £9.5k instead of £6.4k.
