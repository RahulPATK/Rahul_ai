## Key points

- **North America** gave the largest absolute gain: +145 orders, +£4,800 revenue and +290 customers. It accounts for about 75% of the net revenue increase.
- **EMEA** is the one concern: orders -2.5%, revenue -2.0%, customers -0.6%. The -£3,100 revenue drop offsets most of APAC's gain. The data doesn't say why. Worth asking the EMEA lead whether this is a one-off or a trend.
- **APAC and LATAM** both grew faster than the company average. LATAM is the fastest-growing region (+5.2% orders, +4.0% revenue) but is small, at about 8% of revenue.
- **Average order value** slipped slightly (£42.45 to £42.28). Revenue growth is coming from order volume, not basket size. AOV fell in North America, APAC and LATAM, and rose slightly in EMEA (£42.71 to £42.92).

## Caveats

- This is a single week-over-week comparison. Movements of 1–2% at company level may be within normal weekly variation, and the files give no longer history to judge that.
- The exports have no cause or context (promotions, pricing, seasonality, FX), so the points above describe what changed, not why.
- AOV is derived here as revenue ÷ orders and is not a finance-reported figure.
