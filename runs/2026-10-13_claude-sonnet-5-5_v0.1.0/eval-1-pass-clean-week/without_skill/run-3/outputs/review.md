## Key points

- **North America** contributed most of the absolute growth: +£4.8k revenue and +145 orders, about 75% of the net revenue gain.
- **EMEA** fell on orders (-90), revenue (-£3.1k) and active customers (-80). Its average order value rose slightly (£42.71 to £42.92), so the drop is in volume rather than basket size. EMEA is the second-largest region, with about 32% of revenue. Worth asking the regional lead what drove it.
- **APAC and LATAM** grew fastest in percentage terms (revenue +3.7% and +4.0%). LATAM's base is small, at about 8% of revenue.
- **Average order value** fell in North America (£44.28 to £43.90) and LATAM (£36.15 to £35.74). It rose in EMEA and fell slightly in APAC. This is a small effect, but North America is where it is most visible.

## Data check

- Regional exports sum exactly to finance's reported totals for both weeks, on all three metrics. There are no reconciliation gaps.
- Active customers is summed across regions. If a customer can be active in more than one region, the total may overcount. The figure matches finance's reported number, so it is consistent with how they report it.
- The data covers one week of comparison only. It can't say whether the EMEA dip is a trend or noise.

## Suggested discussion

1. Understand the EMEA decline: is it a one-week dip or the start of a trend?
2. Check whether the small AOV decline in North America reflects a mix or promotion change.
