## Observations

- **Every region declined on every metric.** The drop is broad rather than isolated to one market.
- **EMEA and North America account for most of the revenue loss.** Together they are £35,800 of the £50,000 decline (72%). EMEA fell the hardest in percentage terms among the large regions (-12.0%).
- **LATAM is the weakest in percentage terms** on all three metrics (orders -7.3%, revenue -14.7%, customers -1.7%), though it is the smallest region.
- **North America's order volume held up** (-1.2%) but its revenue fell 9.4%, which suggests lower order value rather than lost demand.
- **The customer base is mostly stable** (-0.9%). The revenue decline comes from fewer orders and lower spend per order, not from customers leaving.
