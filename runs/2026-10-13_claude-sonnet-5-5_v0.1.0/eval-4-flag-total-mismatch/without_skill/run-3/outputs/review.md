## Takeaways

- **Revenue fell in every region by 9–15%, much more than orders (-1% to -7%).** Revenue per order dropped from about £42.4 to about £39.2 (-7.7%, regional-sum basis). That points to lower basket size, pricing or discounting, or mix, and not only lower volume. The data here can't tell us which.
- **LATAM is the weakest region** on all three metrics: orders -7.3%, revenue -14.7%, customers -1.7%. It is the smallest region, so it contributes less to the total decline in absolute terms.
- **EMEA is the largest absolute revenue loss**: -£18,300, and the steepest decline among the large regions (-12.0%).
- **North America is the most resilient** on orders (-1.2%) and customers (-0.3%). Its revenue still fell 9.4%, in absolute terms the largest contributor to the decline after EMEA (-£17,500).
- **Active customers are nearly flat (-0.9%).** The customer base is largely intact, but each customer is ordering and spending less.

## Next steps

1. Finance to reconcile the £6,600 revenue gap.
2. Investigate the drivers of the drop in revenue per order (pricing, promotions, mix), especially in EMEA and LATAM.
